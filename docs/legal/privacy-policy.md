# All Document Reader & Editor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

## 1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Dong (“we,” “us,” or “our”), the developer of the All Document Reader & Editor Android application (“All Document Reader & Editor” or the “App”), handles information when you use the App. Contact details appear in Section 16.

All Document Reader & Editor is a document reader, editor, scanner, and PDF toolkit. It supports common document formats and provides features such as document creation and editing, PDF annotation and signing, forms, page organization, conversion, compression, password protection, redaction, OCR, translation, and reading supported text aloud. Feature availability may vary by version and device.

The App is free to download and is supported by advertising. Optional subscriptions or one-time purchases may be introduced or made available through Google Play. This Policy covers purchase-related processing when those offers are available.

All Document Reader & Editor does not require you to create an account with us. Its document tools work on your device; All Document Reader & Editor is not a cloud document storage or backup service. This does not mean that no information leaves your device: advertising, analytics, diagnostics, model downloads, external services, and purchases may involve data transfers described below.

This Policy provides information about our practices. Using the App is not a substitute for any separate consent required by law.

## 2. Information and permissions used by the App

All Document Reader & Editor may access the following information to provide the features you use:

- Documents and file information: document contents, file names, paths, file types, sizes, and modification dates, to browse, search, open, create, edit, organize, save, share, or delete files. Searching document text happens on your device.
- Storage access: on Android 11 and above, the App may request all files access for document management across accessible shared storage. On earlier supported versions, Android storage permissions may be used. Restrictions imposed by Android still apply. If you decline broad storage access, browsing and management may be limited; individually shared or selected files can be opened where supported.
- Camera and selected images: to capture and process scans, import photos, and create documents or PDFs. Camera access requires Android permission. Images you select may be read through Android’s picker or the permission granted for that selection.
- Local preferences and history: language, appearance, selected use cases, sorting, recent searches, recent files, favorites, reading position, bookmarks, and recently used translation languages.
- Network information: connectivity status and technical connection information needed for online services, downloads, advertising, and purchases when available.
- Advertising identifiers and related information: advertising services may access the Android advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, subject to availability, settings, consent requirements, and applicable law.
- Notification permission: if notification features are available, Android 13 and above requires permission before notifications can be shown. You can revoke it in Android settings.

The App does not request microphone, precise location, contacts, SMS, phone, or calendar permissions in its current main application manifest. Advertising and other online services may still infer an approximate location from your IP address without a location permission. Read-aloud sends text to the selected speech engine; it does not require microphone recording by All Document Reader & Editor.

You may change permissions in Android Settings > Apps > All Document Reader & Editor > Permissions, and storage access through the applicable special app access settings. Menu names vary by device. Revoking a permission may limit the related feature.

## 3. Information stored on your device

All Document Reader & Editor stores documents and working data locally as needed, including:

- Original or exported documents, scans, images, and other output files in the locations used or selected for saving.
- Local copies of documents opened through other apps or content providers, so that All Document Reader & Editor can read and edit them. Changes to an imported copy may not update the original file.
- Recent and favorite file records, including file paths and metadata; search history; reading positions and bookmarks; and app preferences.
- Saved signature drawings, stored as image files for reuse. All Document Reader & Editor does not use these drawings to identify you through biometric authentication.
- Drafts, intermediate files, previews, temporary scanner images, translation results, and downloaded language or recognition models as applicable.

App-specific data and files in shared storage have different lifecycles. Clearing app data or uninstalling normally removes app-specific information, while files saved to shared storage may remain. All Document Reader & Editor sets Android’s app backup flag to disabled; device transfer or manufacturer backup behavior can differ. Keep your own copies of important documents.

## 4. Information that may leave your device

### Document tools and model downloads

All Document Reader & Editor’s document editing, scanning, conversion, OCR, and translation tools process their input locally. We do not operate a server to receive your documents for these operations. Google ML Kit processes its supported input on your device, but may connect to Google for models, updates, compatibility information, and usage or performance metrics. These technical transfers differ from uploading your document contents for processing.

### Read-aloud

When you use read-aloud, document text is passed to the text-to-speech engine selected on your Android device. That engine may process text locally or through its provider’s servers, depending on its settings, installed voices, and provider. Review that provider’s privacy policy and use offline voices if you want to avoid network speech processing where supported.

### Analytics, diagnostics, and configuration

The App integrates Google Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, installation-related services, and remote configuration. These services may process app or installation identifiers, device and operating system information, app version, usage events, session information, IP-related connection information, and diagnostic data. The App’s screen analytics records screen identifiers rather than document text.

Crash information may include stack traces, technical messages, and file names or paths contained in an error. We do not intentionally attach document contents or signature images to screen analytics or crash reports. Remote configuration retrieves settings used to control feature availability and app behavior.

### Advertising

The advertising-supported service may display ads through Google AdMob and its advertising partners. Advertising services may collect or receive advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from network information, device and app characteristics, and ad views, clicks, and other interactions. Purposes may include delivering and measuring ads, preventing fraud, and personalizing advertising where allowed.

Ad personalization and other advertising processing depend on your region, choices, device settings, and applicable consent requirements. Non-personalized or limited advertising can still involve data processing; it does not necessarily mean that no identifiers or connection information are used. See Section 6 for available controls.

### Optional subscriptions and purchases

When paid offers are available, Google Play processes purchase and payment information. The App may use product identifiers, purchase tokens, order-related information, entitlement status, and renewal or expiry information to verify purchases and provide or restore eligible access. We do not receive your full payment card number or bank account credentials from Google Play.

A plan removes ads only if ad removal is expressly included in that offer. Removing ads does not automatically disable analytics, diagnostics, model downloads, or purchase verification. Subscription cancellation is managed separately through Google Play and does not itself delete app data.

### Sharing and support

If you share a file, open it with another app, or save through an external provider, the selected app or provider receives the content or access you authorize. A cloud storage provider selected through Android may transfer files under its own policy.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and any information you choose to provide. Avoid sending confidential documents, passwords, payment credentials, or signatures unless necessary for your request. Opening external links or Google Play pages also exposes ordinary connection information to the destination service.

## 10. Deletion and local controls

You can remove documents or saved items through the relevant app controls where available. Removing an item from Recents or Favorites does not necessarily delete the underlying file. Deleting a document does not remove copies previously shared or backed up elsewhere.

Clearing app data normally removes app-specific records, imported copies, settings, and saved signatures. Uninstalling normally removes app-specific data, while shared-storage documents and third-party records may remain. Back up important files before clearing data.
